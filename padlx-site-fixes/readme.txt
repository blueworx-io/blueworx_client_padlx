=== PADLX Site Fixes ===
Requires at least: 6.3
Requires PHP: 7.4
Stable tag: 1.0.1

Small PADLX site changes. Settings are under Settings → PADLX Site Fixes.

== Changelog ==

= 1.0.1 =
* Checkout and the documents email now show the final Founding Membership schedule.
* The under-18 pop-up now uses the final wording.
* The Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy pages can now appear in search.

= 1.0.0 =
* Checkout shows only the schedule for the membership being bought, with a required tick box. Each acceptance is saved on the order and in a record on the settings screen.
* Under-18 pop-up on the founding member sign-up form.
* After a membership purchase, members are emailed the T&Cs, Privacy Policy and their schedule.
